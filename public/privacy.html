Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Linework is a web app for scanning barcodes and recording the values in Google Sheets. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no Linework server that receives, stores, or processes your data.

Barcodes, photos, and the camera

Camera frames and uploaded images are decoded on your device and are never uploaded or stored. Scan results are kept in the page only until you reload or close it.

Google account data

When you choose to sign in with Google, Linework asks for:

  • your email address and basic profile (first name), used only to show who is signed in;
  • access to your Google Sheets, used only to read the spreadsheets you open in Linework, add rows for your scans, and make the edits you request.

The access token Google issues is held in memory in your browser tab only. It is never written to storage or sent anywhere except to Google's own APIs, and it is discarded when you reload the page or sign out. Linework does not read, modify, or share any spreadsheet you have not opened in the app, and it uses Google data for no other purpose. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data stored on your device

To remember your setup, Linework saves the links to spreadsheets you open, your column mappings, and your preferences in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device; use "Forget link" in the app or clear your browser's site data to remove it.

Third parties

  • Google provides sign-in and the Google Sheets API, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
  • The app is hosted on Cloudflare, which may keep standard request logs.

Linework has no analytics or advertising, and it never sells or shares your data.

Removing access

Signing out of Linework revokes its access token. You can remove Linework's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions.
