Privacy policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Linework is the barcode scanner app from stucovitv.in. It scans barcodes, records values in Google Sheets, and helps organizers run events with their people, documents, and alerts. Scanning happens entirely on your device. Linework's server, hosted on Cloudflare, stores only what the features below need.

Barcodes, photos, and the camera

Camera frames and uploaded images are decoded on your device and are never uploaded or stored. Scan results are kept in the page only until you reload or close it, unless you record them in a spreadsheet.

Google account data

When you choose to sign in with Google, Linework asks for:

  • your email address and basic profile (first name), used to identify you in Linework and show who is signed in;
  • access to your Google Sheets, used only to read the spreadsheets you open in Linework, add rows for your scans, and make the edits you request;
  • only if you create a document for an event: access to the specific Google Drive files Linework creates for you, used only to create those Sheets and Docs and to share them with the people of that event.

On the website, Google's access token is held in memory in your browser tab and is discarded when you reload the page or sign out. In the iOS and Android app, Google's sign-in software manages the token and can restore your Google sign-in until you sign out. The token is sent to Linework's server once, at sign-in, so the server can ask Google to confirm who you are; the server does not keep it. Linework does not read, modify, or share any file you have not opened or created in the app, and it uses Google data for no other purpose. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Linework's server stores

  • Your account: your email address, first name, and Google account ID.
  • Sign-in sessions: a cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 12 hours on the website, or a token kept in your phone's secure storage that keeps you signed in to the app for up to 30 days. Only a one-way hash of either is stored.
  • Your change log: each change you make to a spreadsheet through Linework (which spreadsheet and tab, and the values you added, edited, or deleted), so changes made while Google Sheets is unreachable can be added later and you can review your history. Only you can see your change log.
  • Teams you create: the names and email addresses you add to them. Only you can see your teams.
  • Events: their names and descriptions, the names and email addresses of the people added to them, their roles and sub-teams, the Sheets and Docs listed in them, and the alerts sent in them with who has acknowledged each. People in an event can see its member list; documents and alerts are shown only to the people they are for.
  • Notification subscriptions: if you turn on notifications on a device, the address your browser's push service gives Linework. Alert contents are encrypted so that only your device can read them. In the iOS and Android app, the notification address Expo gives your phone; app alerts travel through Expo's push service and Apple's or Google's, and are not end-to-end encrypted.

If you are added to an event by an organizer, your name and email address are stored as part of that event even before you sign in. Signing out of Linework ends your session and turns off notifications on that device.

Data stored on your device

To remember your setup, Linework saves the links to spreadsheets you open, your column mappings, and your preferences in your browser's local storage or the app's local storage. The app keeps its Linework session token in the phone's secure storage. If neither Google Sheets nor Linework's server can be reached, a change you make is kept there until it can be uploaded. Use "Forget link" in the app or clear your browser's site data to remove it.

Third parties

  • Google provides sign-in and the Google Sheets and Drive APIs, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Your browser's push service (for example Google, Mozilla, Apple, or Microsoft) delivers encrypted alerts.
  • In the app, Expo (expo.dev) and Apple's or Google's notification services deliver alerts to your phone.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
  • The app and its database are hosted on Cloudflare, which may keep standard request logs.

Linework has no analytics or advertising, and it never sells or shares your data.

Removing access and data

Signing out of Linework revokes its Google access token. You can remove Linework's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions. You can delete your teams, leave events, and discard stored changes in the app; event owners can delete their events and everything in them. To have your account and all data about you deleted, contact us below.
